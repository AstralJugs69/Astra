# Cell biology

The nucleus stores genetic information and directs the activities of the cell.

The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell.

The mitochondrion produces energy for the cell.

Cells use water and nutrients to support ordinary growth.

The microscope helps a student compare cells at different scales.
